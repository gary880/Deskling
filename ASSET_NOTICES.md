# Asset notices

## Mochi

Generated for the Deskling MVP and stored at `public/pets/mochi/spritesheet.webp`.

Final generation prompt: a strict 8 × 6 production spritesheet of a consistent cream-and-caramel pixel-art cat, with rows for idle, walk, sleep, thinking, talking, and happy animation on a removable chroma-key background. The final asset was converted to transparent WebP using the built-in image generation workflow and local chroma-key removal.
